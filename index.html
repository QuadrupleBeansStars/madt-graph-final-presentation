A complaint is rarely one office’s job

Traffy Fondue lets residents report a broken pavement or a blocked drain in a few taps. The report is then passed through the city administration until someone fixes it.

The platform launched on 31 May 2022 and passed one million resolved complaints in March 20261,2. At that scale, how a complaint travels matters as much as how many arrive.

Our question

Where do complaints get passed around, stuck, or sent back — and which handoffs look wasted?

One row is one path through the city

1 · Read the list. Each chip is an agency, in the order it took part.

2 · Clean it. The root “กรุงเทพมหานคร” is on 98% of tickets so says nothing about routing: drop it. District units such as “ฝ่ายโยธา เขต…” fold into one role, “ฝ่ายโยธา (เขต)”.

3 · Draw it. Every neighbouring pair becomes a directed edge. Repeat for 5,000 tickets and the paths merge into one network.

Positions never move while time runs — items just appear, so you can see where new routes form. The sample is dense only in recent months, so the full range is mostly empty until 2026.

What the graph shows (1): longer routes, fewer finished

Careful. This is correlation. Hard problems may need more agencies and take longer. The graph tells us where to look, not what to blame.

Only tickets at least 30 days old are counted, because a young ticket cannot be “finished” yet.

What the graph shows (2): two-way handoffs and bottlenecks

Pairs that pass tickets both ways

Most “in between” agencies (betweenness score)

    Busy ≠ in the way. A table can count tickets per agency; only a graph can say which agency sits on the most routes.

    What the graph shows (3): vague categories scatter

    A tight category like “Trees” reaches few agencies. “Other” and “Suggestion” fan out across the whole administration — a routing problem a table of categories would never reveal.

    Distinct agencies = agency nodes reached by that category’s tickets (after folding district units).

      Limits we want to be upfront about

      • 5,000-ticket sample, mostly from the last 3 months.
      • Completion rates need ≥30-day-old tickets; counts are small for long chains.
      • Correlation, not cause.
      • forward and follow states are not in the data dictionary; we show them as-is.
      • Names are Thai; English glosses are ours.
