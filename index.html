Traffy Fondue: report a city problem in a chat, track it to the end

A citizen-reporting platform built by NECTEC and rolled out by the Bangkok Metropolitan Administration (BMA) on 31 May 20221. Residents use the LINE chatbot or a website; officials work the cases in a manager app4.

  1. 1ReportA resident sends a photo, a location and a short description.
  2. 2RouteThe platform classifies the problem and sends it to a responsible office4.
  3. 3HandoffIf that office cannot fix it, the ticket moves on to another, and another.
  4. 4UpdateStatus changes (in progress, finished) are sent back to the resident.

The handoff is the subject of this talk. Every office a ticket passes through is written into one field, in order.

A complaint is rarely one office’s job

Handoffs are not rare. A ticket lists agencies on average, listed in order.

The platform passed one million resolved complaints in March 20262. At that scale, how a complaint travels matters as much as how many arrive.

Ch 1–2 · Nodes, edges, direction, weight, time

A graph is nodes and arrows, and we choose what they mean

  • Node = an agency or district office.
  • Edge = one handoff, drawn from the sender to the receiver.
  • Weight = tickets that took that step (line width). Direction matters: A→B is not B→A.
  • Time = each edge keeps a weekly count, so the graph can replay how routing built up.

    Plain RAG over rows

    Retrieves the few rows that look most like the question. Good for “show me a ticket like this”. Cannot count, follow a route, or tell you what is typical.

    Graph RAG

    Retrieves a path or neighbourhood computed over all tickets. Answers about structure — where, how many, who is upstream — come with the edges that support them.

        Appendix · Ch 3 · Graph types

        Which kind of graph? The question picks the type

        TypeUse when…For Traffy
        Directed, weightedWho sends to whom, how muchUsed. Handoffs between agencies, weight = tickets.
        TemporalTiming or evolution is the questionUsed. Weekly counts per edge drive the time slider.
        Bipartite (+ projection)Two node kinds, edges only acrossUsed as a second layer: category → agency (“routed to”).
        MultigraphRepeated interactions carry the meaningCollapsed: repeats become the edge weight.
        Ego networkZoom to one node and its worldAvailable in the explorer: select any node.
        HypergraphA group acts as one unitNot needed: a ticket is a path, not a group.
